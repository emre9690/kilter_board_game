How to play
  1. Connect the board, then press Start and put the phone down.
  2. Get on the wall near the bottom while the timer runs. The board counts down 3 – 2 – 1 – GO.
  3. Bars fall from the top. Lit holds are lava. When a bar reaches you, every hand and foot has to be on a hold in the gap.
  4. Each bar that reaches the bottom is a point. Bars get faster and change color as you go: blue, then purple, then red.
  5. Honor system: if you touch a lit hold or come off the wall, press I'm out. Your score shows on the board.
